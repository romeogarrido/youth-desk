# Youth Desk (demo)

Scouting desk for youth scouts and scouting agencies in Belgium.

- Desk: players, work lists (video, follow-up, live), SOF and NOTE reports by position.
- Agencies: several scouts share one player base; every report is signed by its author.
- Surclassés: players who play above their age group in U18 Elite, U18 Elite 2, U16 Elite and U16 Elite 2 (RBFA match sheets).

This version is a demo: accounts are simulated, players are invented, and changes stay in the visitor's browser.
The Surclassés data is a snapshot of 20 October 2026.

Deploy: static site, no build step and no environment variables (Vercel: import the repo, keep the defaults, Deploy).
